# Treatment: The Five-Dollar Latte

Style: sketch-explainer

Question: a latte sells for five dollars and its ingredients cost about one dollar twenty; where does the rest go?

## Structure (≈ 45 s)
1. Hook (0–8 s): a customer pays five dollars; a sign shows what the cup really costs.
2. Question (8–12 s): so the café keeps almost four dollars? A barista shakes her head.
3. The month (12–35 s): one café, thirty thousand dollars of sales; a board fills slice by slice — ingredients 30%, staff 30%, rent 12%, the rest.
4. The answer (35–45 s): about nineteen percent is left, in a well-run café; most aim for ten to fifteen. The money is in selling many drinks.

## Look
Kit sets: market-stall (café counter outside), street-day, whiteboard-room, chalk-room, living-room as the back office, spotlight for the "not even close" beat, sunset-city to end. Max the guide in every act; a white-faced barista and customers; one board per act. Medium shots about half the time, a close-up on the reaction.

## Sound
Kokoro narrator, Salamander piano bed about 11 dB under the voice, small pops on the board slices, a till sound on the payment.
