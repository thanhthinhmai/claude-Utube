Title: The Five-Dollar Latte
Target: 45s
Language: en
Speed: 150

[P01] A latte costs five dollars. The coffee, the milk and the cup cost about one dollar twenty.

[P02] So the café keeps almost four dollars? Not even close.

[P03] Picture a well-run café. Every month, it sells thirty thousand dollars of drinks and food.

[P04] Thirty percent goes to coffee, milk and cups. Another thirty percent pays the staff. Twelve percent pays the rent.

[P05] Power, marketing, insurance and supplies take a little more.

[P06] What is left? About nineteen percent, in a well-run café. On a five-dollar latte, that is roughly ninety-five cents. Most cafés aim for ten to fifteen percent.

[P07] Every cup pays a little toward the staff, the rent and the lights. So the profit is not in one drink. It is in selling two hundred of them a day.
