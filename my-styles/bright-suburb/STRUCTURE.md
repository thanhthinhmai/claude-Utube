# Film structure (measured)

Measured from the 12 reference transcripts (five sampled windows per video at 0–8 %, ~24 %, ~43 %, ~61 %, ~80 %) plus a transcript of the last 20 % of each video, and from the shot tables. Shares are of total runtime. Only structure is recorded here: no lines, characters, stories or names.

Runtime of the reference set: 10.7–20 min, median ≈ 12.9 min. Studio default for this style: 10–13 min. Compressed form for 4–7 min films: keep every act, scale the shares.

## Acts

| # | Act | Share | Starts at (median) | What happens (pattern) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook + rule | 0–5 % | 0.0 s | Cold open inside an everyday action in second person (first sentence ends ≈ 3.9 s). The power is shown 2–4 times, each bigger, with a short bystander reaction between. Then one plain rule sentence (≈ 32 s) and a fixed four-word "it starts out great" pivot (≈ 36 s, 2.9–5.6 %). No spoken "what if" line, no question to the viewer. |
| 2 | Upside montage | 5–25 % | ≈ 36 s | Small low-stakes wins at home, school or work, each a 2–4 sentence mini-scene opened by a time stamp. |
| 3 | Scale-up | 25–40 % | ≈ 26 % | A fixed "then it grows" step takes the power public: strangers, crowds, media, other towns. First real-world fact aside here. |
| 4 | First cost | 40–55 % | ≈ 43 % | The power hurts someone else or isolates the hero. Told calmly, closed by a one-line sting. |
| 5 | Dark turn | 55–75 % | ≈ 61 % | The hero uses the power on purpose for gain, or a family member pays. Second fact aside often here. |
| 6 | Public climax | 75–92 % | ≈ 80 % | A big public stage (studio, show, arena, ceremony) with the audience size stated; the recurring family member or peer is present; one choice. |
| 7 | Coda + closer | 92–100 % | ≈ 92 % | Quiet resolution scenes, often with one or more time jumps; a family-table or home scene; the opening motif returns. The last sentence asks the viewer the title premise as a "So would you…?" question, starting ≈ 4.9 s before the end (4.3–6.2 s); last word ≈ 1.8 s before the end. |

## Beat grid

- Structural steps at ≈ 5 %, 25 %, 43 %, 61 %, 80 %, then the coda at ≈ 92 %.
- A new mini-scene every 15–25 s; a time-stamp opener ≈ 2.4 times per minute.
- Consequences ladder: self → family → school/work → town → nation/media; stated numbers rise through the film (tens → thousands → millions); ≈ 6 numbers per minute.
- 1–2 real-world fact asides (1–3 sentences, third person) on their own infographic or period set, then straight back to the hero.
- One recurring family member and one peer or love interest come back in every act and stand in the climax.
- A running object or motif from the hook returns 2–3 times and pays off in the coda.

## Sets per act

- Hook: 4–5 sets in the first minute.
- Middle: 3–4 sets per 30 s.
- Climax: 2–3 sets (one big stage plus a cutaway).
- One home set returns in every act (≈ 26 % of shots); 10–20 sets per film.
- Single-location premises keep the same grid, use rooms of one building and day counts.

## Narration rhythm

- Second person, present tense; median sentence 6 words; 21–42 % of sentences ≤ 4 words.
- Pauses ≈ 0.34 / 0.52 / 0.77 s (in sentence / between sentences / between beats).
- Speed flat across acts (≈ 3.6 words/s speaking, ≈ 2.55 words/s overall).
- Character quotes every 2–4 sentences; no rhetorical questions to the viewer before the closer.
