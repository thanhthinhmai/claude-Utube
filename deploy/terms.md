# Terms of use

*Sample text. The studio owner edits this file (`deploy/terms.md`) before opening sign-up, ideally with a lawyer.*

## The service
Film Studio makes short films and learns visual styles for you, using Claude. It is offered for free trial, as is,
with no promise that it is always available or that a result meets your needs. Monthly limits apply and may change.

## Your account
- One person per account. Keep your password private.
- You can delete your account at any time. Everything in it is deleted within 30 days.

## What you may ask for
You may only ask for films you have the right to publish. The studio does not make:
- graphic violence or gore;
- sexual content;
- hate or harassment against a group or a person;
- films that copy or impersonate a real person, a brand, or a channel, or use their name, likeness or logo.

The studio may refuse an idea, stop a job, or lock an account that breaks these rules.

## Learning a style from videos
- When you name a YouTube channel, you confirm that you have the right to use its videos, or that you use them only
  to study their style.
- Reference videos stay private on the studio's server. They are never shown, played, downloaded or shared, even
  with you, and they are deleted when learning ends. Only measurements and a few proof frames are kept.
- A style learns the *look and sound*, not the content: the stories, characters, voices and music of a film are new.

## Your films
The films are yours to use. You are responsible for how you publish them.

## Privacy
The studio keeps your email, your jobs, your films and styles, and the cost of each job. It does not sell them or
share them, except with the services needed to run the studio (email delivery, Anthropic for Claude).

## Contact
Questions: reply to any email from the studio.
