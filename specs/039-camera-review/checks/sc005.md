Draft 3: S1, S3, S7, S11 match (zoom within 1 point, ease within 0.10); the 5 Blender shots keep their own lens camera
(S12 off by 1.4 points). The shots that come out short are the same ones the film's hand camera had to boost with a
per-shot factor `k` (CAMFIT: S5 1.2, S8 1.46, S10 1.18, S13 1.6, S14 1.43, S15 1.16; the passing S7 1.08, S11 0.95).
Measured over keyed zoom in draft 3: S5 1.29, S8 1.81, S10 1.36, S13 1.43, S14 1.31, S15 1.40. In flat cartoon pictures
the track reads less zoom than the keys hold, so keying the option's measured zoom as is gives too little.

**Fix (user choice 2026-11-28: fit after the draft)**: `camera fit <film>` measures the draft, scales each off shot's
keyed zoom (`shot_plan.<S>.camera.fit_k`, 0.5–3; CU.planCam and the Blender lens use zoom·fit_k), drafts those shots
again and measures, up to 3 rounds (proportional first, then the secant through the last two reads: S5 read 6.0 at
×1.0, 9.1 at ×1.30, 6.5 at ×1.11, not proportional). A shot whose read does not move when fit_k changes sets its own
camera and is reported (S12: its Blender script passed its own zoom and parallax 0.6; the kit now takes the plan's lens
zoom whenever a shot passes no move or zoom, its frm/look only place the still camera; the film's S12.py was changed).
